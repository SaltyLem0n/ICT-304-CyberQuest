Ch 5 · Public Key Cryptography & RSA

1. Primes p, q (p≠q) → n = p·q, z = φ(n) = (p−1)(q−1)
2. Pick e: 1<e<z, gcd(e,z)=1 → Public PU=(n,e)
3. Find d: e·d ≡ 1 (mod z) → Private PR=(n,d)
4. Enc (0≤m<n): c = me mod n | Dec: m = cd mod n

Trapdoor: Mult easy, factoring n hard. Textbook RSA keys swap only for raw (me)d ≡ (md)e (mod n).

  • Confidentiality: Enc PUB, dec PRB. Auth/Int: Sign PRA, verify PUA.
  • Hybrid: Random session key K encrypts data; PUB encrypts K. Needs PKI/CA vs MitM.

Modular Inverse (Extended Euclidean: z & e)

Start z=1·z+0·e, e=0·z+1·e. For ri−1=q·ri+ri+1, subtract q×row(i) from row(i−1). At rem=1, coeff of e mod z is d (if <0, +z).

Repeated Squaring (Square & Multiply)

Split exponent into powers of 2 (173=128+32+8+4+1). Square & reduce mod n each step; multiply chosen powers mod n.

Checked Mini RSA Example

Keygen: p=3, q=11 → n=33, z=2×10=20. e=3 (gcd(3,20)=1).
Inv: 20=6×3+2 (1·20−6·3); 3=1×2+1 (−1·20+7·3) → d=7.
Enc m=4: c = 43 mod 33 = 64 mod 33 = 31.
Dec c=31 (d=7=4+2+1): 311≡31; 312≡4; 314≡16 (mod 33).
m = (31×4×16) mod 33 = 1984 mod 33 = 4 (60×33=1980, rem 4) ✓

Pitfalls: Use z ONLY for d; use n for enc/dec! Keep m<n; never pack a whole name > n.

Ch 6 · Digital Signatures & Hash Functions

E-Sig ≠ Digital Sig: Sig = H(M) signed with PRA (Authentic, Unforgeable, Non-Reusable, Unalterable, Non-Repudiable) → Auth+Int+Non-Repud (not confidentiality).

Envelope: EK(M) || EPU_B(K) || EPR_A(H(M)).

Ch 8 · TLS 1.3, PGP, VPN & IPsec

Layers: App: HTTPS(443), SSH, PGP | Transp: TLS | Net: IPsec | Link: PPTP/L2TP.

TLS 1.3 (RFC 8446): 5 AEAD suites; (EC)DHE gives forward secrecy (PSK-only psk_ke & 0-RTT have exceptions; no static RSA kex, RC4, 3DES, CBC, SSL/TLS≤1.1).

PGP: Hybrid + sig via Key Ring & Web of Trust (no CA).

IPsec (L3, IKE): AH (auth+int) vs ESP (enc+auth+int).
